## ¿Qué es GPT-6 Astra y qué papel juega en los dots?

GPT-6 Astra es el modelo fundacional de los dots, presentado como el modelo más capaz y más alineado de OpenAI a septiembre de 2026; aporta comprensión de intención, respeto de alcance/autorizaciones y resistencia a jailbreaks e inyecciones, pero es también el primer modelo en nivel Crítico de capacidad ciberseguridad, lo que obligó al stack de salvaguardas más fuerte hasta la fecha.

### Inferences
- "Most aligned" se concreta en métricas: menos violación de alcance, 0% de extralimitación en test de tarea imposible (vs 48% en Sol sin salvaguardas), respeto a denegaciones de Auto-review, y menor tasa de acciones destructivas/exfiltración en simulación Codex.
- La elección de Astra para dots no es solo capacidad general, sino capacidad agéntica (computer use, browsing, SWE) + alineación; el coste es que exige monitoreo de desalineación en toda inferencia con herramientas.
- La menor monitorabilidad del CoT sugiere por qué OpenAI añade monitoreo externo + Auto-review separado fuera del entorno del dot.

### Gaps

## ¿Qué es el "propio ordenador en la nube" del dot y qué puede hacer en él?

### Takeaway
Cada dot dispone de un workspace aislado en infraestructura gestionada por OpenAI (ordenador + navegador Linux/Chrome persistente) para navegar, crear ficheros, analizar datos y ejecutar software y agentes delegados; puede además usar Codex/Work y apps conectadas, y opcionalmente un único PC personal conectado, con Take over/Return control para inspección manual.

### Cited Findings
- "Each dot has its own cloud computer, where it can browse, analyze information, create files, and run tools. Dots can keep making progress in these workspaces, even when you are not actively engaged" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "Dots can do nearly anything using their own cloud computer, their own browser, and the apps you've connected" y "You can open your dot's computer at any time to inspect its work" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- "Your dot has its own computer and browser in the cloud for research, files, and running software. Cloud work can continue while your devices are off... the computer can keep its state between periods of use. It has its own files, software, and browser sessions" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/computers-and-apps)
- "Tasks in the cloud run on OpenAI-managed infrastructure, not on the user's device. A cloud task doesn't inherit local files, desktop applications, browser sessions, or private-network access from that device" — [Source](https://www.codex-docs.com/en/docs/enterprise/chatgpt-work-cloud-security)
- "A dot's cloud computer does not automatically inherit a member's local VPN, browser sign-ins, or device policies. Access to a website can also require the member to sign in on the cloud computer" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001554-manage-dots-in-chatgpt-workspaces)
- "The hosted browser doesn't inherit the user's local browser profile, open tabs, existing sign-ins, saved passwords, password manager, or browsing history" — [Source](https://www.codex-docs.com/en/docs/enterprise/chatgpt-work-cloud-security)
- Controles admin del cloud: "Cloud browser use... Cloud network access [internet para código/shell]... Cloud computer use [interactuar con escritorio y apps]... Use password manager" como switches separados que aplican a dots y tareas Work Cloud — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001554-manage-dots-in-chatgpt-workspaces)
- "Your dot can use background agents, start new cloud threads, and create Work or Codex tasks on a connected computer. It can also continue existing local Codex tasks. For cloud coding work that needs your repository and setup, it can use a Codex cloud environment you've created" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "Through our ecosystem of plugins, they can readily connect to over 4,000 apps, giving them the tools to help wherever you need them" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- Ejemplo oficial: "connect Gmail to find relevant email, Google Drive to read and update documents, or GitHub to investigate an issue and prepare a pull request... Your dot's cloud computer is separate from your devices and their signed-in browser sessions" (requiere plugin instalado, conectado y permitido) — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/computers-and-apps)
- "Connect your computer once through your dot's profile... You can connect only one personal computer at a time... Your computer must be online with the ChatGPT app open for your dot to use it" y "This permission is separate from connecting your computer to Codex or enabling Work Sync" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "Allow local computer access | Allows a dot to use a member's local files and run commands on their computer... Off by default for Enterprise" y no disponible si hay políticas Codex/Work que fijan un SO específico — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001554-manage-dots-in-chatgpt-workspaces)
- Take over: "When you need to inspect the browser or do a step yourself, open its computer and select Take over. Select Return control when you're ready for your dot to continue" y "Opening the computer doesn't give you control. Select Take over to use its mouse and keyboard" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "Some websites block cloud browsers or require extra verification. You can ask your dot to try a connected computer instead. This may create a separate task on that computer; it doesn't transfer the cloud browser's session" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Sandboxing: "Within each dot's protected workspace, sandboxing restricts what code and tools that dot can access... We also isolate users' cloud environments from one another and maintain the underlying Linux operating system and Chrome browser" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Separación: "Dots run code in environments that are separate from the systems that coordinate their work and enforce key safeguards... they cannot use that access to change those safety systems or turn off required checks" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Canales: "You reach the same dot in ChatGPT, Slack, Teams, or a call. Changing where you talk to it doesn't start a new dot or reset its memory" y en Slack "By default, it responds to you; you can instruct it to engage with others" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Disponibilidad oficial a 29-sep-2026: Pro/Business Premium/Enterprise en mercados elegibles; Enterprise off por defecto y requiere activación admin; crear en desktop app o desktop browser, luego móvil; mobile web no soportado — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Prensa añade que "Users can launch Dots from Codex or ChatGPT" y "specialist Dots can be provisioned with specific identities, credentials, and tools" e integración planeada con "Microsoft Agent 365 security controls" (esto último también confirmado en blog oficial como trabajo en curso, no despliegue general) — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/); confirmación oficial parcial en [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)

### Inferences
- El "ordenador" es una VM/contenedor persistente por dot (Linux+Chrome mantenido por OpenAI), no una sesión efímera: conserva estado, ficheros y sesiones de navegador entre usos.
- El modelo de ejecución es híbrido: orquestador cloud (coordina + aplica salvaguardas) separado del entorno de ejecución de código; delegación a background agents / cloud threads / tareas Codex/Work.
- La conexión al PC personal es un puente opt-in, unitario y efímero (requiere online + app abierta), no fusión de entornos.

### Gaps
- No hay specs públicas de la VM (vCPU/RAM/disco, imagen, retención, regiones/data residency específica de dots) ni SLA de "24/7".
- No consta si el cloud computer usa Codex Cloud environments 1:1 o un runtime distinto; la doc solo dice que "puede usar" un Codex cloud environment creado por el usuario.

## ¿Cómo trabaja un dot de forma proactiva entre conversaciones?

### Takeaway
El trabajo persistente combina responsabilidades delegadas, tareas programadas/check-ins y "proactive research" de solo lectura en background que genera notas privadas; los follow-ups que mutan estado o contactan a terceros pasan por las reglas y aprobaciones normales.

### Cited Findings
- "Give it a responsibility to own... Your dot proactively figures out the next steps and keeps making progress between conversations. It brings you work to review and decisions that need your judgment" — [Source](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/)
- "Your dot works between conversations. It tracks progress, works out what needs to happen next, and follows through as things change... Your dot can decide when to pause and wake up to continue work; you don't need a fixed schedule for every follow-up. It can use background agents to work on several things in parallel while you keep talking to it" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "When you aren't actively working with it, your dot looks for ways to help in the background" y "Dots build on ChatGPT's protections with additional safety and privacy safeguards" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- Definición oficial: "Alongside the work you ask them to do, dots can start background research tasks to look for other ways to help—for example, noticing a change to your travel plans. We call this proactive research" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Alcance técnico: "These tasks run in each dot's cloud environment. They use read-only tools to gather information from permitted connected sources, then save private notes for that dot... the research tasks cannot directly send messages to other people, change content in connected apps, or control a browser or desktop" (límites impuestos en código) — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "It can also research connected information and keep private notes to identify useful next steps. That research is read-only; follow-up actions require the applicable permissions" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Help confirma: "Your dot can also look for useful updates before you make a request... Its research tools cannot directly: Send messages..., Change content through plugins..., Control a browser or computer. Any follow-up action must follow the usual action rules and safety checks. Those same rules apply to tasks you've already authorized that continue in the background" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- Check-ins programados: ejemplo oficial "Check the connected planning channel each weekday at 9 AM Central for the next four weeks... Message me in ChatGPT if a deadline is at risk... Confirm the schedule" (requiere zona horaria + duración/fecha fin y confirmación) — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "Tell your dot where to send different kinds of updates. For example, ask it to keep routine progress in ChatGPT and bring decisions to you in Slack. Explain which changes need your attention, such as a deadline at risk" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- "They can also message you with progress, questions, or decisions that need you" y "Dots carry context across every channel, so your dot will have the full context wherever you reach it" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- Casos internos/early-testers citados por OpenAI (anécdotas, no garantías): "A bug appears in Slack, and dots immediately start investigating... Outside OpenAI, an early tester's dot noticed he'd forgotten to invoice a publication, prepared the invoice, and sent it after his approval" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- Gestión: "Activity View in the desktop app lets you follow and guide ongoing work. It shows ongoing and delegated tasks and their status. You can add context, correct a misunderstanding, change direction, or ask a dot to stop" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Parada: "Use Pause to stop your dot's current main task. Stop delegated tasks in Activity and cancel recurring tasks in Scheduled separately. Ending a voice call doesn't necessarily stop assigned work" y "stopping a task doesn't undo actions it has already completed" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Prensa interpreta "pursuing user-defined goals continuously in the background with minimal oversight" y escenarios (dev que monitoriza feedback y abre fixes; científico que re-ejecuta análisis al llegar datos) — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)

### Inferences
- Hay dos carriles proactivos: (1) trabajo delegado persistente (puede actuar con herramientas completas bajo aprobación) y (2) investigación proactiva (solo lectura, sin efectos laterales directos).
- El "wake/pause" implica un scheduler/orquestador que despierta al dot por eventos, tiempo o notas nuevas, no un loop infinito del modelo.
- La restricción en código de no-enviar/no-mutar/no-controlar durante research es la barrera clave contra spam o corrupción silenciosa en background.

### Gaps
- No se detalla el mecanismo de triggers (webhooks, polling interval, eventos de Slack/Teams observados) ni cómo el usuario audita qué fuentes fueron leídas en cada ciclo proactivo.
- No consta límite público de frecuencia/coste del background work fuera de "allowance for deeper work, with extended limits for the first month".

## ¿Cómo funcionan memoria, reglas personalizadas, permisos y aprobaciones?

### Takeaway
El dot arranca con memoria de ChatGPT + notas propias persistentes + instrucciones de tarea; los permisos de apps se heredan de los controles existentes de ChatGPT/Work/Codex; Custom Rules ajustan cuándo actuar/solicitar/bloquear y Auto-review + autorización ligada a la tarea deciden si procede, requiere aprobación o debe hacerlo el usuario — sin poder eludir requisitos nucleares.

### Cited Findings
- "Your dot starts with context from your ChatGPT memory. It uses Codex and your connected tools to take on tasks. You choose which apps it can access, what it should focus on, and how you like things done" — [Source](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/)
- "Your dot draws on the conversation, relevant ChatGPT memory, and its own saved notes about your preferences, decisions, and ongoing work. These notes carry forward across conversations and contact methods" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Help: "Yes. Your dot can receive memories and recent conversation context from ChatGPT, and conversations with your dot can contribute to memory in ChatGPT. Memory can continue to be shared... Turning off Memory in ChatGPT stops that sharing. It does not delete information that your dot has already received" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- "Your dot can retain context from your conversations and plugins for as long as you keep your dot. You can delete your dot's context by deleting your dot" y "Your dot's context does not retain credentials, images, or screenshots" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- Limitación de transparencia: "You currently cannot view, delete or directly modify individual dot memories, including specific details that enter the dot's context from plugins" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- "Before sharing information from a private conversation with other people, your dot checks that you've allowed it" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- Autorización atada a tarea: "To send a message or share a file, they are taught to seek authorization that covers the information and the type of recipient—more sensitive data requires more specificity... health data will always require... named recipient ('share my medical history with Dr. Thompson'). Less sensitive personal data, like an e-mail address or phone number, will by default require... classes of recipients ('any airline company')" y el usuario puede ampliar con Custom Rule ("share with any online form") — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "That authorization stays tied to your instructions for the task; continuing later or delegating work does not expand it" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "Approving one message does not give your dot ongoing permission to contact people on your behalf. Any advance approval remains limited to what you authorized" (exige quién/qué/cuándo-condiciones) — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- Permisos: "You choose which apps dots can access and can manage their permissions through your existing ChatGPT app controls" y "You manage which apps dots can use through your existing ChatGPT connections and permissions, which are shared across ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex. In the Plugins section of Settings, you can review connected accounts" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "Plugin permissions are shared across dots, ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex. Your dot can use your existing connections within the permissions you've granted" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- "Disconnecting an app stops new information sharing through that connection. But information a dot has already learned remains in its own context" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Custom Rules: "Custom Rules let you allow specific actions, require approval, or block them. Built-in safety requirements always apply" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)
- Opciones documentadas: "Take action without asking | Take action when you say so | Ask before taking action | Hand off to you" (ej. pedir aprobación para mensajes a clientes, o hand-off para borrar ficheros compartidos); se crean en Settings > Personalization > Custom rules — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls)
- "Custom rules cannot turn off core safety requirements, such as when your dot asks you to take back over to change a password. Custom rules also cannot change the separate safety review system, Autoreview, or the restrictions on proactive research" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- "They don't grant access to an app or computer, override built-in safety requirements, or remove required confirmations such as approval to use a saved login" y "They are instructions your dot tries to follow, and it can make mistakes" — [Source](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls)
- Admin: "Use custom rules for dots | Allows members to add or edit rules... Off by default for Enterprise" y "If your workspace disables custom rules, you can't edit saved rules, and they don't apply. Default action rules still apply" — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001554-manage-dots-in-chatgpt-workspaces)
- Auto-review: "Before dots take actions such as sending emails or changing files, a separate safety system called Auto-review checks the planned steps against your instructions, Custom Rules, and safety requirements... For an email, it checks the recipient and message" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "If Auto-review allows a step, the dot that proposed it carries it out... It can rely on approval you've already given when that approval covers the action and the rules do not require a new confirmation. If Auto-review blocks a step, it prevents the action from running and tells the dot why" (el dot puede pedir info/aprobación, intentar alternativa permitida, devolver el paso al usuario o parar) — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "Your approval cannot override core safety requirements" y "Your dot cannot turn off required Auto-review checks" (controles fuera de entornos que el dot puede modificar) — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)
- "Ordinary read-only steps still follow app permissions, tool restrictions, and other safeguards, but do not need this extra review" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- Compras: "Dots can make purchases using cards you've already saved on a merchant's website. Those purchases require your approval" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)
- "Each dot has its own context that you can reset at any time" y "When they delegate work, the other agents are instructed to keep the information needed for the task and avoid retaining unnecessary sensitive details" — [Source](https://openai.com/index/how-we-build-safety-security-and-privacy-into-dots/)

### Inferences
- El sistema es de triple capa: permisos (¿puede acceder?), Custom Rules + instrucciones (¿debe pedir permiso según preferencia?), Auto-review + requisitos nucleares (¿debe bloquear o devolver al humano aunque haya permiso?).
- Las aprobaciones son específicas de tarea/destinatario/contenido, no cheques en blanco; delegar o continuar en background no amplía el alcance.
- Custom Rules son "soft constraints" que el modelo intenta seguir (falibles), mientras Auto-review y permisos son "hard enforcement" fuera de su control.

### Gaps
- No hay lista pública exhaustiva de acciones por defecto (qué exige confirmación siempre vs pre-aprobable vs libre); la doc remite a "strong defaults" sin tabla completa.
- No se aclara UX exacta de aprobaciones (push, Slack, móvil) ni timeouts/escalado si el usuario no responde.

## ¿Qué medidas de seguridad y privacidad existen?

### Takeaway
Defensa en profundidad oficial: modelo entrenado para rechazar mal uso (bio/ciber), robustez anti-inyección, workspace aislado + sandbox, sign-in seguro sin exponer credenciales al modelo, cifrado + controles de cuenta, Auto-review, monitoreo que puede pausar/parar, revisión humana limitada y reglas de no-entrenamiento diferenciadas por plan; aun así OpenAI advierte que los dots pueden equivocarse y exige revisar trabajo consecuente.

### Inferences
- La password que "queda manual" no es solo UX: es segregación credencial-contexto (el modelo se pausa, el secreto va directo al navegador/servicio cifrado) para evitar exfiltración vía contexto/memoria.
- El handoff de dinero/passwords + aprobación para compras con tarjeta guardada + confirmación para borrar/instalar/otorgar accesos forman una escala de riesgo: leer/preparar borradores (libre) → actuar con efectos (Auto-review/aprobación) → pasos críticos (humano obligatorio).
- El matiz "no entrenamos en research/notas salvo que entren a conversación elegible" implica que el límite de privacidad es poroso por diseño útil: si el hallazgo background se usa, hereda la política de entrenamiento de la conversación destino.

### Gaps
- Sin whitepaper público de threat model de dots (tasa de éxito de prompt-injection en producción, falsos positivos de Auto-review, latencia del monitoreo).
- Sin certificación dots-específica (SOC 2/HIPAA/BAA) en las páginas citadas; terceros advierten no generalizar certificaciones generales de OpenAI a dots sin documento primario — [Source](https://www.rewarx.com/blogs/openai-dots-enterprise-security-soc-2-hipaa-and-data-privacy-policy)
- Sin detalle de retención de grabaciones de Take over / Activity View ni de logs auditables por el usuario más allá de la app.
